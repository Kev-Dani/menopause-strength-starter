# Menopause Strength Training Coach: Free Starter

An interactive introduction for women in perimenopause, menopause and postmenopause. It explains how the body changes through the menopause transition, why strength training becomes the priority, and how to get started safely.

The skill is evidence-based and informed by the experience of two real people: Kevin, a canfitpro Personal Training Specialist with about 20 years of coaching experience, and Dani, his client, who began strength training in midlife.

## What is included

- Chapter 1: what is happening in the body, and why the old approach stops working
- A guided, step-by-step introductory conversation
- Essential safety guidance, including when to speak to a health professional
- A short reference list of the research behind the chapter

## How to use it

Install the plugin, start a new chat, and ask something like "I'm in perimenopause and new to strength training. Where do I start?"

## Please note

This is an AI-powered, self-guided coaching skill. You are not communicating with Dani or Kevin, and it does not include personal coaching sessions or direct access to them. It provides educational fitness coaching only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Speak to your physician before starting any exercise programme. No results are guaranteed.
